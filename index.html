Who we work with

Five kinds of client, one standard of care.

Individuals

One-to-one support, on your terms

Counselling and crisis support for people navigating grief, trauma, burnout, or a sudden change in circumstance — strictly confidential, and paced to the person, not a protocol.

Families

Support that holds the whole household

Family therapy and mediation that treats the household as the client, not just the person who called first.

NPOs

Care for people who care for others

Staff wellbeing and psychosocial support for humanitarian and field teams working in high-exposure environments.

Private companies & government

Organisational psychosocial programs

Employee wellbeing, critical-incident response, and workplace trauma support for companies — plus disaster response and community-level psychosocial programs for public agencies.

Our approach

Three principles, held in every engagement.

Trauma-informed

Every practitioner works from an understanding of how distress actually shows up in the body and behaviour — not just in what people say.

Context-specific

A support plan for a field team in a crisis zone looks nothing like one for a family after a loss. We build the program around the setting.

Built to last

We hand over tools, referral pathways and internal capacity — not just a course of sessions that ends when we leave.

Confidentiality is not a policy line for us — it's the foundation.

Everything shared with Crisis of Care is held in strict confidence, in line with POPIA and the ethical codes our practitioners are registered under. What's said in a session — individual, family or organisational — stays between you and your practitioner unless you choose otherwise, or the law requires disclosure to prevent serious harm.
