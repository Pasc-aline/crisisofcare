Volunteer recruitment drive

Crisis of Care (Pty) Ltd, in affiliation with Black Womxn Caucus (BWC), is building a network to deliver crisis, grief and trauma support, and a skills register of people who can contribute their time and expertise. Complete this form to join.

Who can apply

Student social workers, social workers, psychologists, community health care workers, social auxiliary workers, therapists, counsellors and occupational therapists.

Supervisors

We also need experienced supervisors. If you have supervised students, interns, volunteers or practitioners before, please complete Section G.

NB: Completing this form does not mean you are automatically taken as a volunteer. Questions marked * are required. Tick all that apply unless stated.
