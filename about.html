Our story

From a WhatsApp helpline to a registered practice.

Crisis of Care (Pty) Ltd provides integrated psychosocial and psycho-health services to individuals, families, organisations and companies. We were born out of the youth-led non-profit Black Womxn Caucus (BWC), launched in 2019 as the emergency #CrisisOfCare WhatsApp Helpline — free, accessible emotional support for people across South Africa during the COVID-19 pandemic. We later registered as Crisis of Care (Pty) Ltd and remain affiliated with BWC today.

Beyond direct support, we conduct research into workforce and community wellbeing, and deliver advocacy, education and training virtually, digitally and in person. Our vision is to be a leading provider of comprehensive mental health and wellness solutions — transforming workplace, family and community environments into spaces that prioritise psychosocial health.

Our multidisciplinary team is made up of registered social workers, psychologists, psychiatrists, counsellors, occupational therapists, social auxiliary workers, community health care workers and faith-based practitioners, working together across every engagement.
